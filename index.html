About

My core strength is taking a model from a blank page to production. I design architectures from scratch, optimise them for speed and memory (quantisation, TensorRT, ONNX), and ship them where latency, hardware limits and reliability matter. Research at the University of Bonn and an M.Tech in Autonomous Systems keep the models current; industry work keeps them shippable.

I work with cameras, LiDAR, laser range finders, microphones and 3D cameras, and I lead teams as comfortably as I write code. I care most about robots that work in messy, uncontrolled places, where clever engineering beats brute compute.
